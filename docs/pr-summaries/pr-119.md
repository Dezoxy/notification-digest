# PR #119: fix(news-site): a hover-opened popover must not swallow the next click
[View PR](https://github.com/Dezoxy/notification-digest/pull/119) · @Dezoxy · merged 2026-08-27 · `aff0413` · `fix/news-site-hover-click-swallow -> main` · +370/-60 across 10 files

## Description
**Found by driving the deployed site, not by the suite.** With a panel open, clicking a headline was swallowed instead of navigating:

```js
settings.open = true; link.click();
-> { navigated: false }
```

## Why this is a regression #118 caused

That swallow is deliberate and predates this work — an open panel behaves like a modal with an invisible scrim, so a dismiss-tap never *also* activates whatever sat under the finger. It was correct while panels only opened on a **click**.

Hover-open broke the premise. A bubble the pointer merely drifted across is not a panel the reader asked for — and for the 140ms of the close timer it would eat their next click. Reachable with any quick move from the masthead to a headline.

## Fix

Hover-opened panels carry `data-hoveropen`, and the three places that treat an open panel as modal skip a panel wearing it:

- the settings outside-click swallow
- the search outside-click swallow
- the soft-nav bail (`details.settings[open], details.searchpop[open]`)

The first two **still dismiss** on an outside click — they just stop calling `preventDefault`/`stopPropagation`, so the click reaches its target. Dismissing was never the problem; making the reader click twice was.

The attribute is dropped the moment the panel closes (`toggle`) or the reader interacts with it deliberately (`pointerdown` on the summary). From then on it *is* a panel they asked for, and full modal behaviour is correct again.
