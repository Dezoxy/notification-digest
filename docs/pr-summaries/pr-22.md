## Description
## What

Two salience fixes in `prompts/digest.md` — prompt-only, no code changes.

**1. Role-based salience in the output contract.** The "Weight ruthlessly" bullet now instructs the model to weight a statement by whether the speaker can *act* on what it describes — a central-bank chief on rates, a regulator on a rule it enforces, a government on a policy it sets, a company on its own product — over any volume of third-party commentary on the same topic. This captures "important people's statements matter" as a role criterion that survives every election and needs zero maintenance, instead of a name list that goes stale.

**2. Criteria-first news interest filter.** The "Interesting:" paragraph previously used seven lab names as the de-facto filter, so a significant new lab was invisible until someone hand-edited the file and cut a release. The criteria (a genuine capability jump, a shift in who can build what, a real deployment or major funding, landscape-changing policy/business) are now the filter, with the names demoted to an "e.g." parenthetical. The EU AI Act / EU regulation emphasis is deliberately kept explicit — it's a reader-specific priority, not an example.

## Why

Review of the prompt found the lab-name list was the one hardcoded entity list in the system, with the classic rot problem: salience should be computed from criteria, not enumerated as names. Same principle applied to speaker weighting before any name list could creep in.
